Stackly started in 2021 with a stubborn belief: the next great app shouldn't require a computer science degree. Today, 48,000+ makers prove us right every day.

Our Mission

Close the gap between idea and product

In 2021, our founders were running a small agency and watching the same story repeat: brilliant people with real problems to solve, stuck in six-month dev queues and five-figure quotes. The ideas weren't the bottleneck — the tooling was.

So we built the tool we wished existed: a visual builder with real components, a real database, and a real publishing pipeline. Not a prototype toy — a production stack you can drag and drop.

Five years later, Stackly powers client portals, internal tools, storefronts, and startups in 140+ countries. And we're still just getting started.

What We Believe

Values we actually use

Not wall posters — these are the four questions we ask in every product review, hiring loop, and support thread.

Democratize, don't gatekeep

Every feature must make building easier for a non-engineer. If it needs a manual, it's not done.

Ship, then polish

We release small and often. Real user feedback beats perfect roadmaps — for us and for our builders.

Earn trust with defaults

Secure, accessible, and fast out of the box. Our users shouldn't need to know what an SSL cert is.

Makers before metrics

When a decision helps the dashboard but hurts a builder, the builder wins. Every time.

Our Story

From garage to global, in five years

2021

Founded in Austin

Elena and Marcus sketch the first canvas prototype in a garage co-working space and onboard 40 beta users from their old agency clients.

2022

Public launch & seed round

Stackly 1.0 ships with 25 templates and the built-in database. A $4.2M seed round grows the team to 12 and the waitlist to 9,000.

2023

10,000 builders & the marketplace

The template marketplace opens to community creators. Workflow automation launches, and the 10,000th builder publishes an app.

2024

Business plans & London office

Team collaboration, SSO, and custom domains arrive for Business customers. We open our first international office in London.

2025

Series A & 100,000 apps

A $18M Series A funds the edge publishing network. The 100,000th app goes live — a nonprofit's volunteer portal built in three days.

2026

48,000 builders and counting

AI-assisted layouts launch, the Bangalore hub opens, and 48,000+ active builders ship on Stackly across 140+ countries.
