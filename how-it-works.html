The Process

Your path to launch

Each step happens in the same visual workspace — no exports, no handoffs, no context switching.

01

Choose a Template

Browse 120+ industry templates — CRMs, storefronts, portals, dashboards — or start from a blank canvas. Every template ships with pages, components, and a ready-made data model.

02

Customize Your Design

Apply your brand in minutes: colors, typography, logos, and spacing flow through every component automatically. Fine-tune anything with the visual properties panel.

03

Add Components

Drag from a library of 120+ production-grade components: tables, charts, forms, galleries, checkouts, maps. Snap them into place and configure behavior without code.

04

Connect Your Data

Bind components to the built-in database, a Google Sheet, or any REST API. Data flows live: edit a record and every bound table, chart, and card updates instantly.

05

Preview Your Application

Flip between desktop, tablet, and mobile frames without leaving the builder. Share a private preview link so clients and teammates can click through the real thing.

06

Test Your Application

Run through flows with test users and sample data before anyone real sees it. The pre-flight checklist catches broken links, empty states, and missing permissions.

  • Roles & permissions verified
07

Publish Your App

Go live on a global edge network with automatic SSL — on a free appflow.site subdomain or your own custom domain. Every publish is a named version you can roll back to.
