Billing FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Yes — every paid plan starts with a 14-day full-feature trial and no credit card is required. When the trial ends you can subscribe or drop back to the Free plan; your projects stay intact either way.
Yearly plans are billed once, up front, at a 20% discount — Pro works out to $15/month and Business to $39/month. You can switch between monthly and yearly at any time from Billing settings, and we prorate the difference automatically.
Nothing is deleted. Apps above your new plan limit are paused — not removed — and go live again the moment you upgrade or archive other projects. Custom domains fall back to your appflow.site address.
We do. Verified students, educators, and registered nonprofits get 50% off Pro. Reach out through the contact page with proof of status and we'll apply the discount within one business day.
All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe, plus PayPal. Business plans on yearly billing can also pay by invoice and bank transfer.
Every new subscription is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. After that, cancelling stops future charges and your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.
